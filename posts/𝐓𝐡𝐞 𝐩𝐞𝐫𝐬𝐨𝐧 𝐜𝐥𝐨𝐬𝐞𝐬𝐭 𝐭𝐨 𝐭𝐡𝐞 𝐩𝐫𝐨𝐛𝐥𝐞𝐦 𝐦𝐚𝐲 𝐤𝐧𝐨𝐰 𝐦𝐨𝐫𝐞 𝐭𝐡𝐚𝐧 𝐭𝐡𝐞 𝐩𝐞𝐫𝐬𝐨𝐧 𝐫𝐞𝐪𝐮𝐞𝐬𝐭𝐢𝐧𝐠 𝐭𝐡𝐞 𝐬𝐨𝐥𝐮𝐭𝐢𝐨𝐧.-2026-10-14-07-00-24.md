A leadership team may say:

“We need another dashboard.”

A sales manager may say:

“We need another field.”

An operations leader may say:

“We need another automation.”

But then you talk to the person actually doing the work.

And they say:

“Actually, here's what happens every day.”

That's where things get interesting.

Because the person experiencing the process often sees problems that aren't visible from a report or meeting room.

They know where the delays happen.

They know where information gets lost.

They know which steps everyone skips.

They know which workaround keeps the process moving.

Good Salesforce strategy shouldn't only listen to leadership.

It should listen to the people living inside the process.
