Your company owns the Salesforce environment.

But who actually understands it?

Who knows why the architecture looks the way it does?

Who understands the integrations?

Who can explain the important automations?

Who knows which decisions were made and why?

Who can safely make changes?

If the answer to all of those questions is:

“Our consultant.”

Then you may own the system legally…

but you don't fully own it operationally.

A strong consulting relationship should increase your organization's understanding over time.

Not create permanent dependence.

Your consultants should bring expertise.

Your organization should gain capability.

That's a healthier long term relationship.
