Not every request needs to become a Salesforce project.

Not every problem needs automation.

Not every process needs customization.

And not every idea that sounds technically possible is actually a good idea.

A consultant can always find a way to build something.

That's not necessarily valuable.

The valuable question is:

Should we build it at all?

Sometimes the right answer is:

“Let's simplify the process first.”

Sometimes it's:

“Salesforce already does this.”

Sometimes it's:

“An integration would be better.”

And sometimes it's simply:

“I wouldn't build that.”

That's not refusing to help.
