You don't always need a major rebuild to get more value from your CRM. 

Sometimes, the biggest gains come from fixing the fundamentals.

1. 𝐑𝐞𝐦𝐨𝐯𝐞 𝐮𝐧𝐧𝐞𝐜𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐚𝐫𝐲 𝐟𝐢𝐞𝐥𝐝𝐬.
If users don't need a field to do their job or make a decision, ask why it exists in the first place. 

2. 𝐑𝐞𝐯𝐢𝐞𝐰 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐚𝐮𝐭𝐨𝐦𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧.
Old Flows and overlapping processes can quietly add complexity. Keep what creates value and retire what doesn't. 

3. 𝐂𝐥𝐞𝐚𝐧 𝐮𝐩 𝐲𝐨𝐮𝐫 𝐝𝐚𝐭𝐚.
Duplicate records, outdated information, and inconsistent values can undermine even the most sophisticated reporting.

4. 𝐒𝐢𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐢𝐟𝐲 𝐮𝐬𝐞𝐫 𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐟𝐥𝐨𝐰𝐬.
If completing a routine task requires too many clicks, your CRM is creating friction instead of removing it. 

5. Audit your reports.
A dashboard is only useful when the underlying data, definitions, and processes are reliable. 

None of these changes sounds dramatic.

That's exactly the point.
