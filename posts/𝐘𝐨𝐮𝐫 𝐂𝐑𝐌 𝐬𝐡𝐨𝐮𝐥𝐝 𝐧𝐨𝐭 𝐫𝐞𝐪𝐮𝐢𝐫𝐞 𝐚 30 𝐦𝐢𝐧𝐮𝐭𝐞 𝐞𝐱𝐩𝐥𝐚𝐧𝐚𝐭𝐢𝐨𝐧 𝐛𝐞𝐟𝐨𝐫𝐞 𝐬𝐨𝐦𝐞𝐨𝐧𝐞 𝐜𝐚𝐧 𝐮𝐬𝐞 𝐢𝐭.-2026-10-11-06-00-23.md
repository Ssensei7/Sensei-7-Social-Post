Think about that for a moment.

If a new employee joins your team and needs someone to explain:

Which fields matter.

Which buttons to avoid.

Which records are actually trustworthy.

Which automation does what.

Which process is the “real” process.

And which parts of Salesforce they should never touch…

You may not have a training problem.

You may have a 𝐝𝐞𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐧 𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐛𝐥𝐞𝐦.

A good CRM should still require training, of course.

But the system itself should make sense.

The easier it is for people to understand how their work fits into Salesforce, the easier it becomes to adopt.

Complexity is sometimes necessary.

Confusion isn't.
