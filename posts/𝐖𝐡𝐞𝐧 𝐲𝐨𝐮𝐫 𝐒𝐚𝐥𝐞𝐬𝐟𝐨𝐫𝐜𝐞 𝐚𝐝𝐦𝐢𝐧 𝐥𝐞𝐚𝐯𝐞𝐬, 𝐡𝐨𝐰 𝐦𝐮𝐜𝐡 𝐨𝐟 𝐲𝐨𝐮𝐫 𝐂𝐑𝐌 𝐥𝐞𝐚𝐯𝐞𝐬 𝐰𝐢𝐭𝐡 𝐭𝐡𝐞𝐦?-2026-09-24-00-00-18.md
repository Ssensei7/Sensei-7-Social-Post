This is a question more companies should ask.

Because sometimes one person knows everything.

They know why the automation was built.

They know which integration depends on which field.

They know which permission was changed three years ago.

They know which workflow nobody should touch.

They know why the strange workaround exists.

And when they leave…

Everyone discovers how much knowledge left with them.

That's not a people problem.

It's a systems problem.

Your Salesforce environment shouldn't depend on one person's memory.

Good CRM management means building knowledge into the organization through:
✔ Documentation.
✔ Governance.
✔ Clear architecture.
✔ Consistent processes.

And a system that another qualified person can actually understand.

Your people will change.

Your CRM needs to survive those changes.
