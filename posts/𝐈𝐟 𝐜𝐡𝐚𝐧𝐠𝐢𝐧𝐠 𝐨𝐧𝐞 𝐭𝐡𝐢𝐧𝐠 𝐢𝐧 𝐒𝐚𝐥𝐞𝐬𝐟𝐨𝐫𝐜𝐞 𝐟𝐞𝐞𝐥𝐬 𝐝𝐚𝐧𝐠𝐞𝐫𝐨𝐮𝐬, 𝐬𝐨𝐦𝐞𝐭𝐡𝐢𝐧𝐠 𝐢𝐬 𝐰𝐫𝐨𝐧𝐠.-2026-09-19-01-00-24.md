“Can we change this?”

“Probably.”

Then:

“Who built it?”

Nobody knows.

“Can we touch this automation?”

“Better not.”

“What happens if we change this field?”

“We're not sure.”

“Does anyone know how this integration works?”

“Ask John.”

That's not just a technical problem.

That's a business risk.

A CRM shouldn't become so complicated that your organization is afraid to change it.

Good architecture isn't only about making Salesforce work today.

It's about making the system understandable enough that someone can safely work on it tomorrow.

That means thoughtful architecture.

Clear documentation.

Good governance.

Maintainable automation.

And knowledge that doesn't live inside one person's head.
